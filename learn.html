How TokenProp Works

Understanding tokenized real estate, fractional ownership, and community-driven governance

🏗️ What is Tokenization?

Tokenization is the process of converting a real-world asset — like a building, land, or villa — into digital tokens on the blockchain. Each token represents a fractional share of ownership in the property.

Think of it like splitting a $1M property into 10,000 tokens worth $100 each. Anyone can buy 1 or 1,000 tokens, giving them proportional ownership rights.

🗳️ Proof-of-Involvement

Unlike traditional systems where whales dominate decisions, TokenProp separates financial ownership from governance power. Your voting weight is calculated using a dynamic score based on three factors:

Ownership (40%) — How many tokens you hold across properties.
Activity (35%) — How actively you participate in voting and proposals.
Duration (25%) — How long you've been holding tokens without selling.

This ensures that active, long-term participants have more influence than passive whale investors.

POI = (Own × 0.40) + (Act × 0.35) + (Dur × 0.25)

💰 Revenue & Income

Owning tokens does not automatically generate income. Revenue depends on how the property is utilized:

Rental income from developed properties is distributed proportionally. Capital appreciation increases token value over time. The DAO can also vote to lease, develop, or sell.

Platform revenue comes from tokenization fees (2%), marketplace transaction fees (0.5%), and premium services.

🔧 Tech Stack

TokenProp is built with a modern, production-grade stack designed for security, scalability, and transparency:

Storage: IPFS for documents & metadata
